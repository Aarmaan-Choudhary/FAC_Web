## This Year at FAC (2025–26)

### November 2026

- **Partnership with Oak Field Research.** We announced an official collaboration with **Oak Field Research LLP**, a quantitative trading and research firm. The partnership brings practitioner-led cohorts, systematic strategy development, algorithmic backtesting and direct industry mentorship to IITK students.
- **Winter Projects 2026.** Five hands-on projects for the winter break: **Market Making: Inside the Order Book**, **Alpha ARC**, **Algorithmic Trading: From Idea to Backtest**, **All Out of Options** and **PortfolioX**. The recruitment test on 12 November drew a great turnout from the Y26 batch.
- **Market Masters 3.0: The AI Investment Challenge.** Teams stepped into the shoes of investment analysts and built long-term investment theses on **OpenAI, Anthropic and NVIDIA**. With **50+ teams and 130+ participants**, the winners were 🥇 **Alpha Architecture**, 🥈 **A17Z** and 🥉 **Flactuents**.
- **ISTC 2027 Launch Event.** After launch events at IIT Delhi, IIT Bombay and IIT Madras, ISTC brought its launch event to IIT Kanpur on 2 November, with an introduction to market making and a live mock trading simulation.

### October 2026

- **FinQuest: The Finance Bootcamp.** Over two sessions, **100+ participants** explored financial markets, financial statements and ratios, and relative and intrinsic valuation, finishing with live financial modelling and a portfolio assignment.

### September 2026

- **The Wall Street Chit Trading Competition.** **100+ teams** battled it out in LH-16 in a fast-paced, offline trading-pit simulation, buying and selling through price crashes to build the best portfolio.

### August 2026

- **FiFA: Freshers' Intro to Financial Analysis.** Our welcome session for the Y26 batch covered stocks, savings, mutual funds, portfolios, bonds and financial markets through interactive activities and real-world examples.

### June 2026

- **The Sovereign Student, with Stride Exchange.** A five-day, risk-free paper-trading championship (10–14 June) with ₹10,00,000 in demo funds per participant and a total prize pool of **₹2,41,200**. The top 15 traders were guaranteed interviews, with up to 5 paid internships at Stride Exchange.

### May 2026

- **Introduction to Investing, with Upstox.** A session for anyone curious about markets and investing, with a free UpLearn subscription for participants.

### April 2026

- **Summer Projects 2026.** Five projects spanning core finance to quantitative strategies: **Equity Edge**, **Derivatives Odyssey**, **AlphaForge**, **Orderbook Oracle** and **Crypto Crush**.
- **IMC Prosperity @ IITK.** IMC ran an information session on **Prosperity 4**, its global trading challenge, and how to approach it.

### March 2026: Non-Core Weekend, 7th Edition

Our flagship event ran from **27 to 29 March 2026**, spanning quantitative finance, investment banking, Web3 and DeFi:

## Non-Core Weekend (NCW)

Non-Core Weekend, the flagship event of the Finance and Analytics Club, is a high-energy, three-day festival designed to ignite financial literacy across the IITK campus. This dynamic fest draws upon a history of distinguished speakers, such as **Subhash Garg** (Former Executive Director of the **World Bank**), **Dr. C Rangarajan** (19th Governor of the **Reserve Bank of India**), **Ajay Bhushan Pandey** (Chairperson of **NFRA**, Ex-CEO of **UIDAI**), and **Mukesh Kalra** (Founder & CEO, **ETMoney**).

![Non-Core Weekend at IIT Kanpur](/static/inspiration.png)

Throughout the weekend, students engage in a variety of exciting activities, including national competitions, hackathons, and immersive workshops. Topics span critical areas such as **Finance, Quantitative Analysis, Economics, Data Analytics, Machine Learning, Blockchain, and Web3**, ensuring relevance in today’s fast-evolving landscape. In collaboration with corporates like **WorldQuant, Groww, and Solana**, Non-Core Weekend serves as a vibrant platform for students to apply their knowledge, develop practical skills, and network with industry experts.

## Lectures & Workshops

The Finance and Analytics Club at IITK offers an exciting lineup of lectures and workshops throughout the year, designed to empower the campus community with real-world financial and analytical skills.

- We also collaborate with industry leaders such as **Zerodha**, **AlphaGrep**, and **WorldQuant** to bring in expert-led sessions, covering a diverse range of domains.

![A Finance and Analytics Club workshop](/static/clay-mockups.png)

Our workshops are tailored to cater to all experience levels—from introductory sessions for freshers to advanced trading bootcamps. Some highlights include offline chit trading competitions, a hands-on Introduction to **Algorithmic Trading** with Python, and our flagship annual winter camp. This immersive camp engages over 100 first-year students, guiding them through critical areas like **Investment Banking, Quantitative Finance, Financial Markets, and Data Analytics**.

## Hackathons

The Finance and Analytics Club (FAC) at IITK is at the forefront of organizing thrilling hackathons throughout the year, both during our flagship Non-Core Weekend and beyond. We partner with industry innovators like **Zelta Labs** and **AlgoBulls** to craft high-stakes competitions that push the boundaries of finance and technology.

- From the adrenaline-pumping **BTC-USDT Algo-Trading** challenge to our renowned **Case-O-Mania**, a financial case competition that tests strategic thinking, to **Quant Quest** with **AlgoBulls**, where participants develop high-performing alphas—our hackathons offer something for everyone passionate about finance, trading, and data analytics.

![A Finance and Analytics Club hackathon](/static/Hackathons.jpg)

Beyond hosting, our team actively competes in prestigious national and international competitions, including the annual **Inter-IIT Tech Meet**, ensuring we're constantly learning, growing, and making our mark on the global stage.
