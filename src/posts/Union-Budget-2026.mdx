## UNION BUDGET 2026–27

From reforms to the Income Tax Act to a renewed focus on infrastructure, employment and growth, the 2026–27 Union Budget sets out the policies that will shape India's economic path for the coming decade. Here is what changes, and what it means for businesses, markets and individuals.

---

### BUDGET AT A GLANCE

- **Total expenditure**: ₹53,47,315 crore, up **7.7%**.
- **Receipts (excluding borrowings)**: ₹36,51,000 crore, up **10.5%**.
- **Fiscal deficit**: ₹16,96,315 crore, or **5.1% of GDP**.
- **Real GDP growth (FY27)**: **6.5–7.0%** (revised estimate).

---

- **No tax** on income up to **₹7 lakh** under the new regime.

---

### INFRASTRUCTURE PUSH

**Description**: Capital expenditure is set at **₹11.11 lakh crore**, about **3.4% of GDP**, continuing the government's investment-led growth strategy.

#### Key Changes:
- **Railways**: **₹2.4 lakh crore** for railway modernisation.
- **Aviation**: **50 new airports and heliports**.

---

### DIGITAL INDIA AND TECHNOLOGY

- **AI and deep tech**: three Centres of Excellence for AI, and support for deep-tech startups.
- **Digital public infrastructure**: expansion of DigiLocker and UPI, and a digital ecosystem for skilling.
- **Data embassies**: countries can set up data embassies in GIFT IFSC.

---

### HEALTHCARE AND WELLNESS

Health spending is directed at four areas: expansion of **PM-JAY**, new **medical colleges**, **digital health**, and **nursing and pharma**. The **Aspirational Blocks Programme** is also expanded to **500 blocks**.

---

### GREEN GROWTH

- **Green Hydrogen Mission**: a target of **5 MMT of annual production by 2030**.
- **Energy transition**: **₹35,000 crore** for priority capital investment.
- **Vehicle scrappage**: the government will scrap all of its old vehicles (15+ years).

---

### SUPPORT FOR MSMEs

- **Revamped Credit Guarantee Scheme**: a **₹9,000 crore** infusion into the corpus, enabling an additional **₹2 lakh crore** of collateral-free credit.
- **PM Vishwakarma Kaushal Samman**: support for traditional artisans and craftspeople, with the cost of credit reduced by **1%**.

---

### DID YOU KNOW?

- This was the first Union Budget **presented on a Sunday**.
- **Debt-to-GDP** becomes the fiscal anchor, with a target of about **50% (±1%) by March 2031**.
- A **tax holiday till 2047** for cloud services routed through an Indian reseller.
- The **IFSC tax holiday** is extended from **10 to 20 years**, after which income is taxed at 15%.
- **MGNREGA is replaced by VB-G RAM G**, raising the employment guarantee from **100 to 125 days**.
- **Capex** increases by **21%**, targeting **₹16.5 lakh crore**.

---

### THE TAKEAWAY: VISION 2047

The budget is organised around inclusive development, reaching the last mile, infrastructure and investment, unleashing potential, green growth, youth power and the financial sector. Taken together, it is a budget focused on **growth, stability, and a developed India by 2047**.
